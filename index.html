Projects

These projects represent my interests in design, interaction, web development, and creative technology.

EchoBand

EchoBand is a wearable project inspired by echolocation and sensory feedback. It explores how technology can communicate spatial information through vibration.

  • Wearable interaction concept
  • Distance sensing and haptic response
  • Focus on comfort and usability

Mood Fan

Mood Fan is an environmental sensing project that uses an Arduino, a temperature and humidity sensor, and NeoPixels to provide visual and functional feedback.

  • Sensor-based system
  • Color states for environmental changes
  • 3D-printed enclosure improvements

About Me

Who I Am

I am a Creative Technology and Design student with interests in web development, wearable technology, and interactive systems. My work often combines visual design with hands-on experimentation.

I enjoy learning how digital identity can be communicated through layout, typography, color, and structure. For me, design is not only about how something looks, but also about how clearly it communicates and how comfortably people can use it.

Accessibility Notes

  • Semantic HTML for better structure
  • Visible focus states for keyboard users
  • Responsive layout for smaller screens
  • Readable color contrast and spacing
